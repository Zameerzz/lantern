How a university application proceeds

  1. You write. Send your qualification, marks, language level, subject, and intake.
  2. We choose the path. Bachelor or Master. Ausbildung follows a company application instead.
  3. We check the profile. If the path is not realistic, I say so before the plan starts.
  4. You pay once. 16000 rupees, then I write the plan.
  5. You receive a shortlist. Five to eight programmes, each with its real deadline.
  6. You get a document list. Passport, transcripts, language proof, CV, and motivation letter. APS only if your country needs it.
  7. You write. I review. The CV and motivation letter stay in your words.
  8. You submit. On uni-assist or the university portal, in your own name. I do not keep your password.
  9. We check the deadline. Anything still missing is named before the date closes.
  10. The university decides. The visa checklist opens only after a real admission letter.
